About 21C Donggang Rafting

Your trusted rafting partner in Yeongwol since 1996

30 Years of Donggang Adventure

Located in Yeongwol, Gangwon-do, 21C Donggang Rafting has been providing unforgettable whitewater adventures on the Donggang River since 1996. We are one of the most experienced and trusted rafting operators in all of Korea.

Our Eorayeon Course takes you through 12 kilometers of pristine river canyon, widely considered the most scenic rafting route on the Donggang. With Class 2-3 rapids, it offers the perfect balance of excitement and accessibility - suitable for beginners, families, and thrill-seekers alike.

What makes us truly unique is our one-stop system: our on-site restaurant and accommodation are just a 1-minute walk from the rafting launch point. No need to drive around looking for food or lodging - everything is right here.

Frequently Asked Questions

QDo I need to know how to swim?
▼

No! All participants wear life jackets throughout the entire trip. A professional guide accompanies every boat, so non-swimmers can enjoy the experience safely.

QIs it suitable for children?
▼

Children from elementary school age (approximately 7+) can participate when accompanied by a guardian. We provide child-sized safety equipment. The Class 2-3 rapids are exciting but manageable for families.

QWhat should I bring?
▼

Bring a change of clothes (including underwear), a towel, and water shoes or strapped sandals (required). Long sleeves and pants are recommended to prevent sunburn. If you wear glasses, bring a glasses strap. All safety equipment is provided for free.

QCan we go rafting when it rains?
▼

Light rain is fine and can make the experience even more exciting! However, for safety reasons, operations may be suspended during heavy rainfall, flooding, or lightning. Full refunds are provided for weather-related cancellations.

QHow do I get there from Seoul?
▼

By car (recommended): Take the Jungang Expressway to Jecheon IC, then follow signs to Yeongwol and Donggang-ro. About 2.5-3 hours from Seoul. Free parking available.
By bus: Take a bus from Dong Seoul Bus Terminal to Yeongwol Bus Terminal, then a local taxi to our site (about 15 minutes).

QWhat is the cancellation policy?
▼

7+ days before: full refund (minus KRW 10,000 processing fee). 6 days: 90% refund. 5 days: 80%. 4 days: 70%. 3 days: 60%. 2 days: 50%. 1 day: 30%. Same day: 20%. Weather cancellations by the operator receive a full refund.

QIs there a group discount?
▼

Yes! Groups of 20 or more qualify for special group discounts. Please call our main office at 033-373-0272 or reservation line at 010-5483-3812 to discuss your group size and get a custom quote. We frequently host corporate outings, school trips, and large group events.

QWhen is the rafting season?
▼

The rafting season runs from May through September, with peak season in July and August. We operate daily from 08:00 to 18:00 during the season. We recommend booking early for peak weekends as spots fill up quickly.
